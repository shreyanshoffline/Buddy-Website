Three things Buddy does well.

✦

Remembers what matters.

Save a preference, a project, or a fact and Buddy carries it forward so the next conversation starts with context instead of a blank slate.

◌

Use your microphone and Buddy can hear you, respond naturally, and read answers back out loud when voice is easier.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026. Buddy is a desktop app built by Shreyansh Patra.

On your computer

What stays local

Chats, Library items, settings, and your local profile stay on your device. Private and incognito chats follow the controls in the app. Buddy does not sell this data.

Model calls

What leaves your computer

When you chat or talk, Buddy sends the text needed for that reply to Hack Club AI so a model can answer. Web search and weather only send the search or place for that request.

Google

Gmail

If you connect Gmail, Buddy asks Google for read access and draft access only. Buddy can read mail and save a draft. Buddy cannot send mail. The token stays on your computer. Disconnect Gmail in Plugins to stop access.

Voice

Microphone

Voice mode uses the microphone only while you are talking to Buddy. Paid speech models run only if you pick them in the app.

Terms of Service

Last updated: September 12, 2026. These terms cover the private beta and this site.

Beta

This is a testing build

Features can change. Buddy may be wrong, slow, or unavailable. Use it for personal work. Do not rely on it as the only copy of important mail, files, or plans.

You choose

Your responsibilities

Connect Gmail only if you want Buddy to read mail and save drafts. Review drafts before you send them yourself. You can disconnect an integration or quit the app at any time.

Limits

What Buddy does not do

Buddy does not send Gmail. Buddy does not let you buy Pro or MAX from the app in this beta. Those plans are granted by the developer after payment outside the app.

Use

Be decent

Do not use Buddy to break the law, harm other people, or attack systems you do not own. Do not try to unlock paid plans without a grant from the developer.
