Three things Buddy does well.

✦

Remembers what you tell it to keep.

Save a preference, a project, a fact — so the next conversation doesn't start from zero.

◌

Talks when typing's too much.

Use your mic. Buddy listens, replies, and can read the answer back out loud.

◫

Keeps your work in one place.

Every saved chat and answer lives in your Library, easy to find again later.
